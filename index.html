About

I study how liquid fuel breaks up into droplets, evaporates and burns in turbulent flames. My PhD at IIT Kanpur combines interface-resolving simulation of atomization with large eddy simulation of the flame that follows it, and I validate the results against benchmark experiments from the University of Sydney.

Large eddy simulation · Multiple mapping conditioning · Atomization · Turbulent spray combustion · Filtered density function · OpenFOAM

Research

Dense spray flames: from atomization to combustion

A two-stage framework models the Sydney Piloted Needle Spray Burner (SYNSBURN). A Volume-of-Fluid and Lagrangian Particle Tracking (VOF–LPT) simulation resolves the liquid breakup and passes droplet statistics to a two-phase MMC–LES combustion solver as inflow conditions. A sparse set of notional particles keeps the cost well below conventional transported-FDF methods, and results agree with gas- and liquid-phase measurements.

Multi-element injectors for space propulsion

Using VOF–LPT in OpenFOAM with several levels of mesh refinement on an HPC cluster, I studied spray interaction between elements of injector heads of the kind used in the Space Shuttle Orbital Maneuvering System.

Multiple coaxial air-blast atomizers

Ongoing work on spray atomization in a multiple coaxial air-blast atomizer, accepted for oral presentation at ICCEU 2027.
